The problem it solves

A timelapse fails at minute 40, not in the edit. The interval that "feels right" either starves the clip or drowns the card; a shutter half a second too long drops frames; a 360-frame night lapse eats two batteries in the cold. Lapsemath turns the event duration and the clip you want into the exact interval, then checks the shutter against the one-second buffer rule, counts batteries against your own shots-per-battery figure, and converts a slider move into millimeters per frame.
